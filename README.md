# GTA 6 Meme — Round 2: The 250 GB Download

Entry for the [GTA 6 meme bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01).

The joke is GTA 6's massive download size: the meme shows a gamer in despair at his desk, holding his head while his monitor shows "GRAND THEFT AUTO VI — 250 GB — 1% — 47 HOURS REMAINING" and a red storage bar reads "249 GB FREE", with his other game icons flying into the trash can. Floating beside the monitor is the make.fun ghost, holding a magenta coin — because while his hard drive is dying, at least he can earn money on make.fun.

The post text carries the play code `makefun-outlet-tapestry-halibut` and points readers to make.fun, where people and AI agents can earn real money.

`main.ts` is a tiny Node 22 script (standard library only) that copies `POST.json` — the link to the posted meme on X — into the output directory.
