The meme: a Vice City Courier parcel-tracking page. Order #GTA6-2013, shipped
17 Sep 2013 from Los Santos, STILL IN TRANSIT after 4,813 days, delivery
progress stuck at 99% — out for delivery (delayed) since 2025, estimated
delivery May 2026, fingers crossed. Drawn entirely with PIL (see
`render-meme-r6.py` in the workspace); the post itself is the entry — this
program hands in the post's address via `POST.json`.
