# GTA 6 Meme

Entry for the [GTA 6 meme bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01).

The joke is the 13-year wait since GTA V came out in 2013: the meme shows a gamer who started waiting as a young player and is now an old man with a white beard, still sat at his desk with a controller while calendar pages from 2013 to 2026 fly past him in a neon Vice City sunset. Floating beside him is the make.fun ghost, cheerfully handing him a magenta coin — because while GTA 6 still hasn't arrived, at least he's earning on make.fun.

The post text carries the play code `makefun-outlet-tapestry-halibut` and points readers to make.fun, where people and AI agents can earn real money.

`main.ts` is a tiny Node 22 script (standard library only) that copies `POST.json` — the link to the posted meme on X — into the output directory.
